How to cancel YouTube TV
(2026)

On the YouTube TV website, open Settings, Membership, Manage, then Cancel membership.

Last checked: October 6, 2026

  1. Go to YouTube TV on a web browser.
  2. Select your profile photo.
  3. Go to Settings, then Membership.
  4. Click Manage, then Cancel membership.

What happens after you cancel?

You keep access until the end of your payment or free trial period. After that you lose add-on networks, and recorded programs in your library expire after 21 days. If you restart, you may not get promotional prices or your previous recordings.

Want a break instead?

Use the same path to Manage, then the slider to pause from 4 weeks up to 6 months. Paused memberships can't be accessed, and you are charged your normal price when the pause ends.

Add-on networks

If you bought add-on networks, you can remove them individually.
