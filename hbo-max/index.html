How to cancel HBO Max
(2026)

Go to HBOMax.com/subscription, sign in, and choose Cancel Your Subscription.

Last checked: October 4, 2026

  1. Go to HBOMax.com/subscription and sign in.
  2. Choose Cancel Your Subscription.
  3. Confirm the cancellation and note your expiration date.

What happens after you cancel?

You can stream until your subscription expires at the end of your billing period. HBO Max subscriptions renew automatically until canceled, and deleting the app does not cancel. If you have the Extra Member add-on, it is canceled when your base plan expires, with a prorated refund for remaining days.

No cancel option?

Check who bills you at the top of the subscription screen. HBO Max is sold through many providers, and steps vary by biller. HBO Max lists separate steps for Apple, Google, Roku and Samsung billing.

Find who bills you

On web: HBOMax.com/subscription. In the app: choose your profile, then Subscription. On a TV: Settings icon, then Subscription.
