How to cancel Disney+
(2026)

Log in on a computer or mobile browser, open Account, and select Cancel Subscription.

Last checked: October 6, 2026

  1. Log in to DisneyPlus.com on a computer or mobile browser.
  2. Select your Profile, then Account.
  3. Under Subscription, select your Disney+ subscription.
  4. Select Cancel Subscription.

What happens after you cancel?

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current subscription term. Timing varies by plan and whether you pay monthly, annually, or are in a promotional period. Canceling does not delete your Disney+ or MyDisney account.

Billed by someone else?

If you signed up through a third-party billing partner, the steps vary. Use that partner's help center or contact them.

Want a break instead?

Monthly subscriptions billed by Disney+ can be paused: Account, your subscription, Pause Subscription, then choose how long. During a pause you are not charged and have no access. Some plans are not eligible. Pausing Disney+ also pauses other services in a bundle.

Source: Disney+ Help Center: How to cancel Disney+. For account-specific problems, contact Disney+ or your payment partner through their official site.
