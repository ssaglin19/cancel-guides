How to cancel Paramount+
(2026)

The steps depend on where you signed up. On paramountplus.com: username, Account, Cancel subscription.

Last checked: October 4, 2026

  1. Visit paramountplus.com and select your username in the upper right corner.
  2. Select Account.
  3. Scroll down and select Cancel subscription.

Signed up on a TV or game console?

Smart TVs (LG, Vizio, Samsung) and consoles (Xbox, PS4, PS5) use the same website steps above.

Signed up through Apple, Google, Amazon or Roku?

Paramount+ says you must cancel on that platform. On iPhone or iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Paramount+, Cancel Subscription. On Android: g.co/play/subscriptions, Paramount+, Cancel subscription. On Fire TV: amazon.com/appstoresubscriptions, Actions, Cancel your subscription. On Roku: highlight the channel, press *, Manage Subscription, Cancel Subscription. On Prime Video: Account & Settings, Your subscriptions, find the Paramount+ channel.

Can't log in?

Paramount+ suggests using the chat on its Help Center homepage.

Source: Paramount+ Help Center: How can I cancel my Paramount+ subscription?. For account-specific problems, contact Paramount+ or your payment partner through their official site.
