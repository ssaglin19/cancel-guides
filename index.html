How to cancel Netflix
(2026)

Open Manage your membership, select Cancel, then Finish Cancellation.

Last checked: October 6, 2026

  1. Open Netflix's Manage your membership page. Sign in if needed. Use the link in the official Netflix cancellation guide.
  2. Select Cancel.
  3. Under Cancel, select Finish Cancellation.

Netflix sends a confirmation email to the address on your account. Signing out or deleting the app does not cancel your membership.

What happens after you cancel?

You can keep watching until the end of your billing period. You won't be charged again unless you restart your account. If you have a gift card or promotional balance, access continues until that balance runs out.

Can't find the Cancel option?

You'll need to cancel with your payment partner. Open your Netflix Account page and look under Membership for a cancellation link or instructions to contact that partner.

Want a break instead?

Eligible accounts can select Pause for 1 Month instead. Billing resumes automatically after the pause, so choose cancellation if you want to stop future charges. Pause is not available on every plan or payment method.

Source: Netflix Help Center: How to cancel Netflix. For account-specific problems, contact Netflix or your payment partner through their official site.
