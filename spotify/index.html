How to cancel Spotify Premium
(2026)

Go to Manage your plan on your Spotify account page and select Cancel subscription.

Last checked: October 6, 2026

  1. Go to Manage your plan on your Spotify account page.
  2. Select Cancel subscription.

What happens after you cancel?

Premium stays until your next billing date, then your account switches to free. You keep your playlists and saved music, and can still log in and play with ads. If you cancel during a zero-priced free trial, the account switches to free immediately and the trial can't be reactivated.

Family or Duo plan

The plan manager cancels the plan. Members who follow these steps only remove themselves from the plan.

No option to change plan?

Your plan is probably billed through a partner such as your mobile or internet provider. On Manage your plan, check Payment for their contact link.

Source: Spotify Support: How to cancel Premium plans. For account-specific problems, contact Spotify Premium or your payment partner through their official site.
