How to cancel Hulu
(2026)

Open your Account page, select Cancel under Your Subscription, then Cancel Subscription.

Last checked: October 4, 2026

  1. Head to your Account page on a computer or mobile browser.
  2. Select Cancel under Your Subscription.
  3. If offered a pause, select Continue to Cancel to keep going.
  4. Select Cancel Subscription.

What happens after you cancel?

Your Account page should read "Your subscription will be canceled," and Hulu emails a confirmation. You keep access until the end of your current billing cycle and are not charged again. Hulu + Live TV free trials end immediately on cancellation.

Billed by a third party?

If you pay through a third party, cancel with them. Hulu's help center covers this separately.

Account vs. subscription

Canceling does not delete your Hulu account or any Disney account you use for other products.

Source: Hulu Help Center: How to cancel Hulu. For account-specific problems, contact Hulu or your payment partner through their official site.
