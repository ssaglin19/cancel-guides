How to cancel Peacock
(2026)

Log in to your account and select Cancel All Subscriptions.

Last checked: October 6, 2026

  1. Log in to your Peacock account.
  2. Select Cancel All Subscriptions. This cancels your base plan and any add-ons.
  3. Follow the steps. You should see a confirmation message and get a confirmation email, which may take a few hours.

Do I get a refund?

Peacock says no refunds or credits are issued for prior payments when you cancel Premium or Premium Plus, unless required by law. Your plan does not renew at the end of its current term.

Only want to remove an add-on?

On the Subscriptions tab, select the add-on, then Remove Add-On.

No cancel option?

You may be billed by a third party such as Apple, Google, Roku or Amazon. Peacock can't cancel those. Contact the third party directly. You can see how you are billed under Payment Method on the Subscriptions tab.

Source: Peacock Help: How do I cancel my Peacock plan?. For account-specific problems, contact Peacock or your payment partner through their official site.
