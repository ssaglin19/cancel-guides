How to cancel Audible
(2026)

On the Audible website, open Membership details, select Cancel membership, then Confirm cancellation.

Last checked: October 7, 2026

  1. On desktop, select your name in the top navigation. On mobile web, open the main menu, then your name.
  2. Select Membership details.
  3. Select Cancel membership.
  4. Select Confirm cancellation.

What about my credits?

For memberships billed directly by Audible, unused credits are lost along with member benefits at the end of your final billing cycle, so use them first. Credits received through an App Store or Google Play Store membership remain in your account after cancellation. Titles you already got with a credit or card are yours to keep. If you would rather keep your credits, Audible says you may be eligible to pause instead.

Can't see the cancel option?

Check you are signed in to the account with the active membership. You may have more than one account.

Billed through Apple or Google?

These can't be canceled on the Audible site. Cancel in the App Store or Google Play Store. Deleting the app does not cancel your membership.

Source: Audible Help Center: Cancel membership. For account-specific problems, contact Audible or your payment partner through their official site.
