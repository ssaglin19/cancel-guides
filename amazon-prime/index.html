How to cancel Amazon Prime
(2026)

Go to Amazon's Cancel Your Prime Membership page and follow the prompts.

Last checked: October 4, 2026

  1. Open Amazon's official Prime cancellation page and sign in if asked.
  2. Select Cancel Your Prime Membership.
  3. Follow the prompts to confirm.

Do I get a refund?

Amazon says paid members who haven't used their benefits are eligible for a full refund of the current membership period. Refunds are processed in three to five business days.

Other subscriptions

Subscriptions on Prime Video, like Paramount+, continue after your Prime membership ends. Review them separately in Memberships and Subscriptions.

Can't cancel here?

If you signed up through Google Play on Android, cancel through Google subscription services. If Prime comes with another company's service, contact that company. Amazon also offers help through Customer Service: choose Something else, then I need help with something else.

Source: Amazon Customer Service: How to Cancel Amazon Prime. For account-specific problems, contact Amazon Prime or your payment partner through their official site.
